Our tours are either riding journeys or riding excursions:

Riding Journeys: Travel through Iceland on horseback, following a continuous route across changing landscapes, from one destination to the next. Experience the adventure of travelling with the horses, discovering new places and sharing the journey along the way.

Riding Excursions: Explore a particular corner of Iceland through different rides, discovering its landscapes, trails and local attractions, returning to the comfort of a familiar base each evening.

Please do not hesitate to contact us at multidaytours@ishestar.is should you have questions or need further information.
